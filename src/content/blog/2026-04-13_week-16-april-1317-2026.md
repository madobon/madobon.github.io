Based on the image provided, here is the step-by-step solution to find the area of the shaded region:

**1. Analyze the Geometry**
*   The outer shape is a square with a side length of **10**.
*   Inside the square, there are two unshaded semicircles.
*   The diameter of each semicircle corresponds to the side of the square, which is **10**. This means the radius ($r$) is half of that, which is **5**.
*   The shaded region is the area of the square minus the area of the two semicircles.

**2. Calculate the Area of the Square**
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{square}} = \text{side} \times \text{side} $$
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{square}} = 10 \times 10 = 100 $$

**3. Calculate the Area of the Two Semicircles**
Since there are two identical semicircles, their combined area is equal to the area of one full circle with radius $r = 5$.
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi \times r^2 $$
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = \pi \times 5^2 $$
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} = 25\pi $$

*(Using $\pi \approx 3.14$)*
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} \approx 25 \times 3.14 = 78.5 $$

**4. Calculate the Shaded Area**
Subtract the area of the circles from the area of the square.
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{shaded}} = \text{Area}_{\text{square}} - \text{Area}_{\text{circle}} $$
$$ \text{Area}_{\text{shaded}} = 100 - 25\pi $$

**Final Answer:**
The exact area is **$100 - 25\pi$**.
Using $\pi \approx 3.14$, the approximate area is **$21.5$**.
