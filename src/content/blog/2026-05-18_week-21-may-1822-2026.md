The correct answer is **A) 14**.

**Explanation:**  
According to the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS), multiplication is performed before addition.  
1. Calculate $3 \times 4 = 12$.  
2. Then add $2 + 12 = 14$.  

Common mistakes might lead to choosing B) 20 (adding first: $2+3=5$, then $5 \times 4=20$), but this violates the correct order of operations.
