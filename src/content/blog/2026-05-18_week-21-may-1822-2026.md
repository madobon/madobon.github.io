The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width. 

**Given:**  
Length = 24 cm  
Width = 12 cm  

**Calculation:**  
Area = Length × Width  
Area = 24 cm × 12 cm = **288 cm²**  

**Answer:** The area of the rectangle is **288 square centimeters**.
