Based on the geometric properties shown in the image, we can solve for $x$ using the concept of similar triangles.

**1. Identify Similar Triangles:**
The image displays two triangles that meet at a central vertex. The lines forming the "X" shape are straight transversals intersecting between two parallel vertical lines (the bases of the triangles).
- The angles at the central intersection are **vertically opposite**, so they are equal.
- Assuming the vertical lines on the left and right are parallel, the **alternate interior angles** are equal. For example, the angle at the top-left vertex is equal to the angle at the bottom-right vertex.
- Therefore, the triangle on the left is **similar** to the triangle on the right.

**2. Determine Corresponding Sides:**
Because the triangles are similar, the ratio of their corresponding sides is equal. The correspondence is determined by the straight lines passing through the center:
- The side labeled **12** (top-left segment) is on the same straight line as the side labeled **$x$** (bottom-right segment). Thus, these are corresponding sides.
- The side labeled **16** (bottom-left segment) is on the same straight line as the side labeled **15** (top-right segment). Thus, these are corresponding sides.

**3. Set Up the Proportion:**
We can set up a ratio of corresponding sides:
$$ \frac{\text{Top-Left Side}}{\text{Bottom-Right Side}} = \frac{\text{Bottom-Left Side}}{\text{Top-Right Side}} $$

$$ \frac{12}{x} = \frac{16}{15} $$

**4. Solve for $x$:**
Cross-multiply to solve the equation:
$$ 16 \cdot x = 12 \cdot 15 $$
$$ 16x = 180 $$
$$ x = \frac{180}{16} $$

Simplify the fraction (divide numerator and denominator by 4):
$$ x = \frac{45}{4} $$
$$ x = 11.25 $$

**Answer:**
The value of $x$ is **11.25**.
