Let the number be $ x $. According to the problem:

$$
x = \frac{x}{2} + 12
$$

**Step-by-Step Solution:**

1. Subtract $ \frac{x}{2} $ from both sides:
   $$
   x - \frac{x}{2} = 12
   $$

2. Simplify the left-hand side:
   $$
   \frac{x}{2} = 12
   $$

3. Multiply both sides by 2:
   $$
   x = 24
   $$

**Verification:**  
Half of 24 is 12. Adding 12 gives $ 12 + 12 = 24 $, which matches the original number.

**Answer:** The number is **24**.
