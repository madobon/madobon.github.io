Based on the image provided, here is the step-by-step solution to find the area of the shaded region.

**1. Identify the Equations**
*   **The Curve:** The graph shows a parabola with its vertex at the origin $(0,0)$ passing through points like $(1,1)$ and $(2,4)$. This is the standard quadratic function: **$y = x^2$**.
*   **The Line:** The shaded region is bounded on top by a horizontal line passing through $y = 4$. So, the equation is **$y = 4$**.

**2. Find the Points of Intersection**
To find the limits of integration, we set the two equations equal to each other:
$$x^2 = 4$$
$$x = \pm 2$$
So, the region spans from $x = -2$ to $x = 2$.

**3. Set Up the Definite Integral**
The area $A$ is the integral of the upper function minus the lower function from the left boundary to the right boundary.
*   Upper function: $y = 4$
*   Lower function: $y = x^2$

$$A = \int_{-2}^{2} (4 - x^2) \,dx$$

Because the graph is symmetric across the y-axis, we can also calculate the area from 0 to 2 and multiply by 2:
$$A = 2 \int_{0}^{2} (4 - x^2) \,dx$$

**4. Calculate the Area**
$$A = 2 \left[ 4x - \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_{0}^{2}$$

Substitute the upper limit ($x=2$):
$$A = 2 \left( \left( 4(2) - \frac{2^3}{3} \right) - (0 - 0) \right)$$
$$A = 2 \left( 8 - \frac{8}{3} \right)$$

Find a common denominator:
$$A = 2 \left( \frac{24}{3} - \frac{8}{3} \right)$$
$$A = 2 \left( \frac{16}{3} \right)$$
$$A = \frac{32}{3}$$

**Answer:**
The area of the shaded region is **$\frac{32}{3}$** (or approximately **10.67**).
