To solve the equation $3x + 5 = 20$ for $x$, follow these steps:

1. **Subtract 5 from both sides** to isolate the term with $x$:  
   $$
   3x + 5 - 5 = 20 - 5
   $$  
   Simplifies to:  
   $$
   3x = 15
   $$

2. **Divide both sides by 3** to solve for $x$:  
   $$
   \frac{3x}{3} = \frac{15}{3}
   $$  
   Simplifies to:  
   $$
   x = 5
   $$

**Verification**: Substitute $x = 5$ back into the original equation:  
$$
3(5) + 5 = 15 + 5 = 20 \quad \text{✓}
$$

**Final Answer**:  
$$
x = 5
$$
